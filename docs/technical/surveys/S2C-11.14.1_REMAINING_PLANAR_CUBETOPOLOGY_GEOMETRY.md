# Remaining planar CubeTopology geometry reconstruction study

S2C-11.14.1. Investigation and reconstruction design only. No CubeTopology identity gained runtime support.

This report is the durable QUALIFIED evidence for the study. Machine output used while gathering it lives under gitignored `tmp/generated/s2c-11.14.1/` and is not required to interpret these conclusions. No Keen assets, vertex tables, or generated CAD are committed.

## 1. Scope

After S2C-11.13.1, Salvador still has 191 CubeTopology instances / 15 identities unresolved. This unit studies the remaining **non-curved** subset:

- 115 instances
- 13 unique identities

RoundSlope (44) and RoundCorner (32) remain explicitly excluded from construction work. They were inspected only enough to prove they stayed excluded and still require curved primitives.

The goal is to answer what solids those 13 identities represent, which families are distinct, whether they can be reconstructed with the existing native procedural architecture, and whether enough evidence exists to authorize a later implementation unit.

This unit does **not** add support. QUALIFIED here means the study is complete and reproducible, not that any remaining identity is supported.

- `CubeSize` Large

Heavy/light HalfSlopeInverted share the same token and the same side *roles* (Heavy* plate prefix). Heavy/light HalfSlopeBox do the same. That is sharing evidence, not a closed-solid proof.

HalfSlopeBox is **not** qualified Slope2Tip. Discriminators:

- token `HalfSlopeBox` versus `Slope2Tip`
- slab plate `SlopePlateSlab` versus `SlopePlateSlope2Tip`
- PatternSize 1×1 versus 4×2 on the slope plate
- Bottom mount half-cell (`EndY=0.5`) versus full-cell (`EndY=1`)

HalfCorner and HalfSlopedCorner share two plate stems (`CornerTriangleLeftPlate`, `HalfCornerDiagonal`) but differ on the top plate (`HalfSlopedCornerBaseTop` versus `HalfSlopedCornerTop`) and mount count (4 versus 6). They are not the same family.

SlopedCornerBase / SlopedCornerTip / SlopedCorner have 7 / 4 / 6 sides and different mount-face sets. Related naming is not geometric identity.

SlopeTransitionTip and SlopeTransitionTipMirrored name each other in `MirroringBlock`, use distinct tokens, and have asymmetric mounts (Front versus Back; Left/Right plate swap). That is chirality evidence, not a vertex proof.

InteriorWall `IsAirTight=true` with six InteriorWall* plates and zero mounts. It is not armor SquarePlate geometry.

| `RoundSlope` | yes | tiles only | Slope-like occupancy + `IsRounded` |
| `RoundCorner` | yes | tiles only | Corner-like occupancy + `IsRounded` |

The 2018 `GetTopologyInfo` table stores, per face: local matrix, normal, FullQuad, IsEmpty, IsRounded, and edge points. That is mount/texture/connectivity metadata. It is not a closed CAD solid grammar. Even for tokens that appear there, SE2CAD already reconstructs Box/Slope/Corner/InvCorner/Slope2Base/Slope2Tip from a separate engineering vertex convention, not by interpreting Sides as a solid.

Current ModAPI `MyCubeTopology` lists the later tokens (and additional tokens not on Salvador). `GetTopologyInfo` still exists in that API. The compiled game almost certainly has later tables. Those tables are **not** in the public 2018 source snapshot, and this operator install copy has no `Sandbox.Game.dll` / `VRage.Game.dll` to inspect.

Therefore:

1. There is no authoritative later-token vertex/triangle/planar-tile table available to this unit.
2. The 2018 table that does exist is not geometry-defining for a closed solid.
3. An exact closed CAD solid cannot be reconstructed from available topology tables for the 13 remaining planar identities.
4. Convexity, half-cell coordinates, and chirality cannot be proven from missing vertex sets.

Do not infer a solid from a subtype name or from a ModAPI enum member existing.

## 9. Proper-rotation equivalence

Existing 24 determinant +1 orientations were applied to qualified vertex sets.

| Family A | Family B | Proper-rotation equivalent | Reflection equivalent | Separate reusable part |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Box | Slope / Slope2Base / Slope2Tip / HalfBox | no | no | yes |
| Slope | Slope2Base / Slope2Tip / HalfBox | no | no | yes |
| Slope2Base | Slope2Tip / HalfBox | no | no | yes |
| Slope2Tip | HalfBox | no | no | yes |
| Slope2Tip | its own X-reflection | **yes** | yes | **no** |
| any qualified | `HYP_SlopeCapYLe0` | no | no | yes |

Slope2Tip therefore does not need a mirrored sibling part. That result does **not** transfer to SlopeTransitionTip / Mirrored, whose vertex sets are unknown.

Later-token pairs cannot be proven equivalent or chiral from vertex-set comparison. Side counts, mount faces, and `MirroringBlock` are circumstantial only.

## 10. Mirror / chirality findings

`LargeBlockArmorSlopeTransitionTip` and `LargeBlockArmorSlopeTransitionTipMirrored`:

- distinct tokens
- explicit reciprocal `MirroringBlock`
- Front versus Back mounts
- Left versus Right slope-2 plate
- distinct Mirrored* triangle plate MWMs

Confidence that they are a chiral pair: **HIGH_CONFIDENCE**.

Proof that the mirrored solid is unreachable by the 24 det +1 orientations: **not available** (no vertex sets). Therefore this is **not** `IMPLEMENTATION_READY_SEPARATE_MIRROR`.

IR remains determinant +1. This unit did not introduce a reflection transform.
